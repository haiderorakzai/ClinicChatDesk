FAQ

What clinic owners usually ask first.

Can we keep our existing WhatsApp Business number?

Eligible WhatsApp Business App numbers can be connected through Meta’s supported onboarding flow. Availability depends on Meta account and number eligibility.

Can our staff still reply manually?

Yes. ClinicChatDesk includes human takeover so staff can pause AI for a conversation and step in whenever needed.

Does the AI give medical diagnosis or treatment advice?

ClinicChatDesk is positioned as an administrative receptionist. Medical decisions and clinical advice remain with qualified clinic staff.

What does Meta charge?

Applicable WhatsApp Business messaging fees are charged by Meta according to Meta’s current pricing rules. ClinicChatDesk does not add a markup to Meta’s charges.

Can we change prices, hours and services later?

Yes. Clinic information can be updated from the clinic dashboard and is used by the receptionist after it is saved.

What happens when the AI is unsure?

Clinics can use safety handoff and human takeover so sensitive or uncertain conversations can be handled by staff.
