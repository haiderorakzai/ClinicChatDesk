Connecting WhatsApp…
Complete the Meta window. ClinicChatDesk will finish the connection automatically.

What happens when you connect?

1
Continue with Meta

Sign in and choose your clinic’s WhatsApp Business account.

2
Choose your number

Use an eligible existing WhatsApp Business App number or set up a new one.

3
ClinicChatDesk connects automatically

Meta returns the authorized account details; no IDs, tokens or developer setup are required from you.

Your Meta access token is exchanged server-side and encrypted before storage. ClinicChatDesk never shows it in the browser.
WhatsApp messaging charges, when applicable, are billed by Meta according to the clinic’s WhatsApp Business usage. ClinicChatDesk does not receive or store the clinic’s Meta payment-card details.
