Privacy Policy — ClinicChatDesk Last Updated: August 20, 2026 ClinicChatDesk (“ClinicChatDesk,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides an AI-powered communication and appointment-management platform that helps clinics manage patient enquiries, WhatsApp conversations, appointment bookings, follow-ups, and related administrative tasks. ClinicChatDesk is operated by Coders Insight Technologies (Pvt) Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use ClinicChatDesk, including our website at https://clinicchatdesk.com, our clinic dashboard, and services connected to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp. 1. Information We Collect We may collect information from clinics, clinic staff, and individuals communicating with clinics through ClinicChatDesk. Clinic and Account Information When a clinic creates an account, we may collect: Clinic name Owner or administrator name Email address Phone number Country Currency Timezone Clinic address Business hours Services and prices Doctor or staff information Account login and authentication information Patient and Conversation Information When ClinicChatDesk is used to communicate with patients or prospective patients, we may process: Name Phone number WhatsApp identifier Message content Voice notes and their transcriptions Appointment requests Appointment date and time Requested services Conversation history Booking status Follow-up status ClinicChatDesk is primarily intended for administrative and receptionist-related communication and is not intended to act as a medical diagnosis or treatment system. Clinics should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive medical information into ClinicChatDesk. 2. WhatsApp and Meta Data ClinicChatDesk may integrate with the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. When a clinic connects its WhatsApp Business account, ClinicChatDesk may receive and process information provided through Meta APIs, including: WhatsApp Business Account identifiers Phone Number IDs Business account information Incoming and outgoing messages Message delivery status User phone numbers Message timestamps Media or voice notes sent through WhatsApp We use this information only to provide the ClinicChatDesk service requested by the clinic. ClinicChatDesk does not sell WhatsApp user data. Use of WhatsApp is also subject to Meta's and WhatsApp's applicable terms and privacy policies. 3. Artificial Intelligence Processing ClinicChatDesk uses artificial intelligence services to assist with functions such as: Answering patient enquiries Providing clinic-approved service information Assisting with appointment booking Understanding conversation intent Transcribing voice notes Identifying potential follow-up opportunities Assisting with cancellation recovery Escalating conversations to clinic staff when appropriate Information necessary to perform these functions may be sent to third-party AI service providers, including OpenAI. ClinicChatDesk is designed so that clinic-specific information such as services, availability, pricing, and business rules can be retrieved from the clinic's own configured data rather than relying solely on AI-generated information. AI responses may occasionally be inaccurate. Clinics remain responsible for reviewing their configuration and handling communications that require professional medical judgment. 4. How We Use Information We use information to: Provide and operate ClinicChatDesk Authenticate users Process clinic and patient conversations Manage appointments Provide AI-assisted receptionist functionality Send and receive WhatsApp messages Process voice-note transcription Recover potential lost bookings Help refill cancelled appointment slots Provide customer support Improve platform reliability and security Detect abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access Monitor service usage Maintain platform performance Comply with applicable legal obligations We do not use patient information for unrelated advertising. 5. Clinics are responsible for: Having an appropriate legal basis for processing patient information Providing any required privacy notices to patients Obtaining consent when required Ensuring their use of ClinicChatDesk complies with applicable healthcare and privacy laws Avoiding unnecessary collection of sensitive medical information 6. What we process

ClinicChatDesk may process clinic account information, business configuration, patient contact details, conversation content, voice-note transcripts, messaging metadata and appointment information to provide the service.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the clinic receptionist, route conversations, create appointments, identify administrative follow-up opportunities, refill cancelled appointment slots, provide support, secure accounts and maintain the service.

AI and voice processing

When AI features are active, relevant conversation context and clinic-approved business information may be sent to the configured AI service provider. If the clinic enables voice-note assistance, incoming audio may be temporarily retrieved and sent to the configured transcription provider so a text transcript can be produced. This starter is designed not to intentionally persist the downloaded raw audio file; the transcript and messaging metadata may be stored according to the configured retention period.

Automated follow-up

Clinics are responsible for configuring lawful and appropriate patient communications, consent practices and approved messaging templates where required by the messaging platform or applicable law.

Retention

Conversation retention is configurable by the platform operator. Clinics should avoid using ClinicChatDesk as a medical record system unless appropriate legal, contractual and technical safeguards have been completed.
