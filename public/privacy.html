Privacy Policy — ClinicChatDesk Last Updated: August 20, 2026 ClinicChatDesk (“ClinicChatDesk,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides an AI-powered communication and appointment-management platform that helps clinics manage patient enquiries, WhatsApp conversations, appointment bookings, follow-ups, and related administrative tasks. ClinicChatDesk is operated by Coders Insight Technologies (Pvt) Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use ClinicChatDesk, including our website at https://clinicchatdesk.com, our clinic dashboard, and services connected to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp. 1. Information We Collect We may collect information from clinics, clinic staff, and individuals communicating with clinics through ClinicChatDesk. Clinic and Account Information When a clinic creates an account, we may collect: Clinic name Owner or administrator name Email address Phone number Country Currency Timezone Clinic address Business hours Services and prices Doctor or staff information Account login and authentication information Patient and Conversation Information When ClinicChatDesk is used to communicate with patients or prospective patients, we may process: Name Phone number WhatsApp identifier Message content Voice notes and their transcriptions Appointment requests Appointment date and time Requested services Conversation history Booking status Follow-up status ClinicChatDesk is primarily intended for administrative and receptionist-related communication and is not intended to act as a medical diagnosis or treatment system. Clinics should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive medical information into ClinicChatDesk. 2. WhatsApp and Meta Data ClinicChatDesk may integrate with the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. When a clinic connects its WhatsApp Business account, ClinicChatDesk may receive and process information provided through Meta APIs, including: WhatsApp Business Account identifiers Phone Number IDs Business account information Incoming and outgoing messages Message delivery status User phone numbers Message timestamps Media or voice notes sent through WhatsApp We use this information only to provide the ClinicChatDesk service requested by the clinic. ClinicChatDesk does not sell WhatsApp user data. Use of WhatsApp is also subject to Meta's and WhatsApp's applicable terms and privacy policies. 3. Artificial Intelligence Processing ClinicChatDesk uses artificial intelligence services to assist with functions such as: Answering patient enquiries Providing clinic-approved service information Assisting with appointment booking Understanding conversation intent Transcribing voice notes Identifying potential follow-up opportunities Assisting with cancellation recovery Escalating conversations to clinic staff when appropriate Information necessary to perform these functions may be sent to third-party AI service providers, including OpenAI. ClinicChatDesk is designed so that clinic-specific information such as services, availability, pricing, and business rules can be retrieved from the clinic's own configured data rather than relying solely on AI-generated information. AI responses may occasionally be inaccurate. Clinics remain responsible for reviewing their configuration and handling communications that require professional medical judgment. 4. How We Use Information We use information to: Provide and operate ClinicChatDesk Authenticate users Process clinic and patient conversations Manage appointments Provide AI-assisted receptionist functionality Send and receive WhatsApp messages Process voice-note transcription Recover potential lost bookings Help refill cancelled appointment slots Provide customer support Improve platform reliability and security Detect abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access Monitor service usage Maintain platform performance Comply with applicable legal obligations We do not use patient information for unrelated advertising. 5. Clinic Responsibilities A clinic using ClinicChatDesk generally determines what patient information is collected and how ClinicChatDesk is used. Depending on applicable privacy law, the clinic may act as the data controller, while ClinicChatDesk may act as a data processor or service provider acting on behalf of the clinic. Clinics are responsible for: Having an appropriate legal basis for processing patient information Providing any required privacy notices to patients Obtaining consent when required Ensuring their use of ClinicChatDesk complies with applicable healthcare and privacy laws Avoiding unnecessary collection of sensitive medical information 6. Information Sharing We may share information with service providers only when necessary to operate ClinicChatDesk. We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, court order, or valid governmental request. 7.
